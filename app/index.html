    The road to the platinum

    Around 200 hours, difficulty about 4 out of 10. It is long, not hard: no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals. The steps below are in the order that wastes the least time.

      What can be missed

      GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Almost everything stays open after the credits — except the two things below.

      Save rules that keep one playthrough enough

      1. No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you cannot select it again. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one at the start of each chapter and an untouched one from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.
      2. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time). She heads out to the balcony of Jason’s house to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends.
      3. Do Race Against Yago the moment it appears. This Chapter 3 race in Yago’s street racing strand is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race.
      4. Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.

      Save rules and the Yago warning come from Output Lag’s guide (community guide, not yet checked in the game). Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist.
